Physical allocation state provenance (original C23232.EXE)

The driver at2aa80 clears69 DWORDs, [7c520,7c634). This is not an
implementation array. Twelve counters have subsequent accesses:7c520,
524,528,52c,534,538,53c,540,544,548,62c,630. The remaining57 words
have no observed identity and are omitted. The contextual statistics
structure retains those twelve counters without reproducing native gaps.
Counter names awaiting their producing pass keep an offset suffix.

Address and escape audit:

An unaligned four-byte-window scan of every file-backed mapped PE section
for ANY value in [7c520,7c634), including interior byte addresses, finds
18 occurrences in .text and none in .rdata/.data/.rsrc/.idata/.reloc.
The occurrences are instruction operands at2aa92,2aafa,2ab04,2ab0a,
2ab42,2ac33,2b7f5,2b819,2bb94,2c2e8,2c3c7,2c596,2c778,2eaf7,
2ec90,2ed20,2ed63,2f005. Each was inspected in instruction context.
They are the clearing address, direct accesses to the listed counters,
and the strategy counter base7c62c. Strategy is0/1, derived from the two
iteration choices; its index cannot reach the cleared gap.

The only address-producing use of7c520 is MOV EDI,7c520 at2aa91,
followed by ECX=69/EAX=0 and REP STOSD. EDI is not stored, passed or
used as a pointer afterward. The other7c520 operand updates its counter
directly. There is no escaping pointer or interior pointer to this span.

The proof also considers base-plus-index/displacement and pointer walks,
rather than equating absence of exact-address references with deadness:

* The preceding register-head array7c4a4 has8 DWORD entries, explicitly
  cleared by2f76f with ECX=8, ending at7c4c4. Its indexed uses are2fc0d,
  3001b,307f7,30ccb. The explicit index loop at3001b is0..7. Other uses
  select a physical integer color from the assigned variable flags, or
  the eight-entry register permutation at7c480. These late allocation
  passes operate after successful coloring, where color32 is unassigned
  and cannot be an allocated physical register. Register24 is resolved
  to its assigned physical color first. There is no negative displacement
  or pointer walk from those heads into the statistics. Reaching7c520
  would require the invalid physical color31 (and larger indices would
  remain outside that array's contract).
  Specifically,2fb85..2fba9 and30c56..30c7a reject physical numbers8..31,
  plus4/5, before those helpers can form an indexed pointer. The sole
  exception is logical frame number24, which is resolved through its
  canonical variable before indexing. Thus the apparently broad direct
  register case later in those functions cannot pass physical31 onward.
* The x87 permutation7c480 is eight WORDs; its pointer loops end7c490,
  with separate marker words7c490/494. The preceding28-byte records are
  eight entries ending7c494. Neither their loops nor indexed accesses
  overlap7c520. Scalar state7c498..7c4a0 does not escape to statistics.
* The following head array starts7c634. It is cleared with ECX=31 at
  2e70b/2e8b7. Its indices are zero-extended register WORDs (2e7c0,
  2e82f and corresponding later consumers), or nonnegative bit-mask
  enumeration. The explicit loop2e867..2e897 uses0..7. Pointer walks
  add4 from7c634 to7c654. There are no signed negative register indices
  or backward walks into the preceding counters. Later pointer chains
  traverse allocated nodes rather than subtracting from the head base.
* The further physical cost array7c6b4 has31 DWORDs, initialized at
  2bd45. Its indices are physical colors/zero-extended register numbers;
  it has no negative displacement or pointer escape into statistics.

The above is a proof under the recovered valid register/index contracts,
not a promise to preserve native out-of-bounds behavior for corrupt IR.
The entire function/callee graph and all twelve contextual counter
updates are retained as their producing functions are reconstructed.
