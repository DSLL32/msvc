Combined enum/type and literal reconstruction
============================================

One user-authorized ordinary C case, /nologo /c /Z7 /O2 /G5 /W3 combined.c.
It forces enum-typed globals/parameters with negative and wide values, a
long-named record whose CodeView fields exceed264 bytes, union/bitfields,
adjacent narrow/wide strings, octal/hex/control escapes and escaped lines,
floating arithmetic/conversion and aggregate initializers. Actual CL child
transport and full file inventories are frozen before reconstruction.
Relative paths are used; there is no path/pointer/clock normalization.
Explicit original captures are outside normal native fixture execution.
